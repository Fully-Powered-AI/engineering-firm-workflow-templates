# Project experience sheet

For one finished project and one proposal, turn the firm's approved project records into a one-page experience sheet: the firm's role, the scope it performed, partners' work kept apart, and the record behind each line. Names, images and permissions still to confirm are listed for sign-off.

*Workflow template · Engineering firms · Marketing and content · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/project-experience-sheet/)*

## The outcome

A proposal asks for a relevant example of your work, and you have the project: finished, filed, and remembered differently by each person who worked on it. You get a project sheet that says only what your records support. It states the firm's role as the agreement gave it, lists the scope your firm performed, and shows in a separate place the work that partners performed. Beside each line sits the record it comes from.

The sheet makes no claim about how the finished design performs and adds no result nobody can show. It contains no engineering judgment. Where a name, an image or a client's permission is still open, the sheet uses a plain description, and a list says what is left to confirm and who confirms it.

The project principal checks that every line is the firm's own contribution. Marketing approves each name, image and claim. Nothing goes into a proposal before both have done so.

## Who starts the work, and when

The proposal lead or a business development coordinator starts it when a request for qualifications or a proposal asks for a relevant project, and runs it once for each project the team wants to show. The project principal verifies the firm's contribution. A marketing lead approves names, images and wording.

The workflow template prepares. It contacts no client and no partner, sends nothing, and changes no record.

## What they supply

- **The request:** what the proposal asks the example to show, and the length the sheet may run.
- **The statement register:** one row for each fact you may want on the sheet, with its category, its source record, its date, who performed the work and whether it may be used. Default headers are in the template.
- **The permissions register:** for the client's name, the project's name and location, each image and each partner's name, whether permission is granted, pending, refused or not yet asked.
- **Your settings:** the firm's name for the sheet, the people who verify and approve, and the length limit.

## What comes out

One document with these parts:

1. **The sheet:** the firm's role, the project at a glance, the scope the firm performed, the work performed by others, the supporting records and any image, each line tagged to the register row behind it.
2. **Fit to this request:** each requirement the proposal states, the rows that match it, or "No matching record".
3. **A list for the project principal:** every line to confirm as the firm's contribution, change or remove.
4. **A list for marketing:** every name, image and permission, and the lines that read as claims.
5. **Not used,** with the reason for each row, and **decisions for people.**

Good looks like this: a reader can point at any line of the sheet and find the record it rests on, and nothing on the page is the firm taking credit for a partner's work.

## What has to change for your company

- **Who verifies and who approves.** Name the principal for each project and the marketing role that approves names, images and claims.
- **Your register.** Keep the default headers or change them, and decide which project records may be cited.
- **Your length.** The default is 250 words; set the limit your proposals use.
- **Your categories.** The default categories are role, scope, size, dates, completion, recognition, performance and other. Rename or extend them to fit your records.
- **Your credit lines and image rules,** if clients or photographers attach conditions.
- **Your platform.** It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, from records you supply. Decide which records the assistant may read and where drafts are kept.

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
